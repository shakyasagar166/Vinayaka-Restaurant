Kitchen & Operations

Kitchen Display System (KDS) & Real-time Stock Control

📋 Live Kitchen Display Screen

Chef ke samne kitchen me tablet ya screen lagi hoti hai. Kaunsi table se kya order aaya hai, kitni quantity hai, aur customer ka special note kya hai - sab transparent rehta hai. Paper KOT kho jane ka dar hamesha ke liye khatam!

🔴 1-Click "Sold Out / In Stock" Control

Agar Malai Chaap ya Dal Makhni khatam ho gayi, to manager mobile se 1 second me use "Sold Out" mark kar sakta hai. Customer wahi order karega jo kitchen me banna sambhav hai. Customer narazgi zero!

🔄 3-Step Lifecycle Workflow

Waiter ya cashier 1 click me status update karta hai: Start Cooking ➔ Mark Served to Table ➔ Mark Paid & Completed. Kaun si table ka bill pending hai, sab screen par saaf dikhta hai.

🗑️ Permanent Delete Cancelled Orders

Jo orders cancel ya reject ho gaye, unhe 1 click me permanently delete kar sakte hain, ya "Clear All Cancelled" se poora screen fresh rakh sakte hain.
