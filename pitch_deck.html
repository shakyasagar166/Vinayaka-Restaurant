Owner's Biggest Concern Solved

Captain 1-Click Confirmation: 'Ghar Baithe Fake Order Ka Ilaaj'

🛡️ Khana Tabhi Banega Jab Waiter Table Confirm Karega!

Agar koi shaitani customer QR code ki photo khinch kar ghar chala jaye aur wahan se prank order kare, to restaurant ko 1 rupaye ka bhi nuksan nahi hoga! Jab tak restaurant ka captain table par customer ko dekhkar 1-Click "Accept" nahi karega, tab tak kitchen me khana banna shuru hi nahi hoga!

Step 1: Ghanti Baji 🔔

Counter aur Captain ke phone par chime baji: 'Table 4 par Shahi Paneer order hua'. Admin screen par golden alert aaya.

Step 2: Table Check 👁️

Captain/Waiter ne Table 4 par nazar daali ki sach me family baithi hai ya nahi.

Step 3: 1-Click Decision ⚡

Family baithi hai to 'Accept & Send to Kitchen' dabao. Table khali hai to 'Reject (Fake Order)' dabao!
